SCOUT Reporter Suite — Change Log

Nordstrom DC499 · Finalized major changes only, newest first

Tuesday, Oct 20, 2026

  • Ecom Backlog v1.7: Expedite orders close to their deadline now show where they are: the pick task and shelf, the tote number, the putwall cubby, or the cubby LPN and where it sits. Orders that were already picked no longer look untouched, and orders whose pick was cancelled with nothing open are flagged in red. 2DD orders near their deadline now have their own list on the 1DD Focus tab. Click an order to see each unit.
  • All reports (OneDrive): A small colored light in the bottom-right corner shows which computer is updating the numbers and how fresh they are. Green or blue = up to date. Red = the numbers are more than 10 minutes old.

Monday, Oct 19, 2026

  • Ecom v3.2: Replenishment no longer counts fills into Retail shelves (F1H, P1H, F2H, P2H). Those fills were also counted on Reserve, so Ecom replen looked much higher than it was. Expect a much lower Ecom replen number from now on.
  • Ecom v3.3 + Reserve Live: Retail and Ecom replen and putaway are now split by how each shelf is set up in MA (Retail or Ecom), not by the letter in the location name. A shelf added or re-zoned later is sorted correctly with no update to SCOUT. Today's numbers do not change.
  • Ecom v3.4: Putaway no longer counts putaways into Retail locations (R1H, R2H, F2H, P2H). That work belongs to Reserve and was counted on both pages. Expect Ecom putaway to drop by about one in five scans on days with Retail putaways.
  • Receiving Live: Updates again every few minutes. It had fallen far behind because each update took several minutes; it now takes about a second, with the same numbers.
  • Retail Backlog: Updates about 3 times faster. Stores with the same order count now stay in the same order between refreshes.

Friday, Oct 16, 2026

  • Backlog Live (Tasks tab): Completed now counts tasks finished this shift even if they were released before the shift started. Before, a batch released just before 2 PM never showed as completed, so the count could sit at 0.

Thursday, Oct 15, 2026

  • Nail the Plan (OneDrive): New version that runs from a shared OneDrive folder instead of GitHub Pages (test run). Every leader sees every department's numbers as they are entered. You pick your department and shift when you open it, and only that one is editable; the rest are view only. Override switches which one you edit and tags the numbers you change. There is a view-only mode, and you can look back at earlier days. The NAIL buttons in Ecom and Reserve open this version when those pages are opened from OneDrive. The old Pages version still works.

Wednesday, Oct 14, 2026

  • Ecom Backlog v1.6: New "Splits" view on the Expedite tab. It shows Ecom orders that were packed into more than one box this shift, how many extra boxes that used, and how many orders would have fit in one box. A 14-day chart shows split orders per day, and the list shows the last 7 days. Tap an order to see each box and what is in it.
  • Ecom Backlog v1.5: New "1DD Focus" view on the Expedite tab. It shows 1DD orders due at the next 12 PM cutoff, orders that are late or less than 2 hours from cutoff, how many made the last cutoff, the average time from pack to pallet, and packed boxes that sit more than 1 hour without a pallet. 1DD Ship By times now count to the 12 PM cutoff, not 24 hours from the order.

Tuesday, Oct 13, 2026

  • Reserve (Live tab): Numbers no longer drop to zero or disappear for a few minutes. When the system was slow to answer, one type of work (for example Pick F2 or Replen) showed 0 for everyone until the next update. Now it keeps the last good numbers until fresh ones come in (Oct 13: this happened about 30 times today).

Monday, Oct 12, 2026

  • Ecom (Live tab): Replen now matches the Full Report. Live counted the drop into P1-PK and skipped the fill from P1-PK to the shelf. It now does the opposite, like the CSV upload (Oct 12 roster: 1,406 before, about 1,297 after).
  • Ecom (Full Report): The Shift Summary tiles and the Activity by Hour chart now match the department cards. Replen counted a case 2 times when it was dropped at P1-PK and then filled to the shelf (Oct 12: tile showed 2,055, the real number was 1,293).
  • Ecom Backlog (Retail Replen Watch): Now lists any LPN sitting at D1-SN-01 with units that are not allocated to an order, with its items and how long it has sat. The list only shows when there is at least one.
  • Ecom Backlog (Expedite tab): Now shows every open 1DD and 2DD order placed in the last 5 days, not only orders placed this shift. Before, the tab started empty every shift while hundreds of expedite orders were still waiting (Oct 12: 122 shown, 939 really open, including 1DD orders from the day before). Shipped now counts orders that shipped this shift.
  • Retail Backlog v1.2: The Units view now has a Ready column. Units on lines that haven't been given stock yet were left out before, so each wave's Total Units was too low (the Oct 12 wave was short 3,388 units).
  • Receiving Live: A system account (manhedgeuser) no longer shows as a receiver, and its LPNs no longer count in the team's hourly totals. After 10 PM the page now says 2nd shift instead of 3rd, to match the numbers it shows.
  • Item Prep Live: The hour columns now add up to each person's total. A carton that was both received and had its condition code removed counted 2 times in the total but only 1 time in the hours.
  • Shipping Live: Containers scanned after the last hour column (for example after 10 PM) now get their own column, so the hours add up to each person's total.
  • Open Totes: Fixed the hospital putwall oLPN list, which was always empty because of a data error. The hospital putwall hasn't been used since March, so the list will stay empty until it is used again.

Monday, Oct 5, 2026

  • Open Totes v1.6: On the TV, when pickers have nothing left to drop, their side of the screen now rotates every 10 seconds through other work that's waiting — totes aging at each location (D1-SN-01 stays in the bottom bar so other spots get the room), orphaned totes, and hospital oLPNs — instead of showing an empty check mark. It still says "Pickers all clear" so pickers know it isn't theirs. The check mark only shows when nothing anywhere needs attention. Each putwall tile also shows how many totes are sitting in its drop zones. Totes that finished picking only show as "not dropped" after 5 minutes, so pickers on their way to the drop zone aren't flagged. Also fixed the TV screen getting cut off on the right when the aging list was long.
  • Ecom Backlog v1.3: Floor Check's Dead Shelves list can now be printed as a worksheet for a team member. It prints whatever is filtered on screen (for example, never-picked items in one area), sorted by location, with the SKU, UPC, description, units, last picked date, a reserve spot that already holds the item, a check box and a space to write the quantity moved. A Lines box sets how many to print (empty prints all) and shows about how many pages that is. Printouts are now solid black on white no matter which theme is picked (dark themes were printing faint). Empty Locations can be printed the same way — for example, a list of shelves holding more items than allowed, with every item's SKU, UPC, description and units.
  • Ecom Backlog v1.4: The Next Batch bar now only counts orders that can actually go into a batch. Orders stuck waiting on a short shelf show in a red "blocked" tag next to it instead of filling the bar. Floor Check also stopped missing orders the system keeps re-assigning — they're now aged from the first time they got stock, so they show up instead of always looking brand new.

Friday, Oct 2, 2026

  • Open Totes v1.6: New TV Mode button for the floor TV. It fills the screen with big, readable numbers: totes pickers still need to drop on the left, putwalls and which drop zone to feed on the right (a mixed drop zone flashes yellow and black), and aging at location, orphaned, hospital and backlog along the bottom. It uses only solid, high-contrast colors so it stays readable on TVs that wash out or over-color the screen. Totes stuck 24+ hours move to Orphaned so they don't keep the pickers' side red all shift. If the data stops updating, the screen dims with a "Data not updating" warning. Press Esc to go back to the normal view.
  • Ecom Backlog v1.3: The No Task tab has a new Dead Shelves view. It lists items on Ecom pick shelves that haven't been picked in 30 days, with the ones blocking an order right now at the top, then items crowding busy shelves and shelves that are completely idle. GWPs are tagged, and each list can be copied as a move list. A third view, Empty Locations, shows how many Ecom pick locations are empty in each zone and aisle, with the open spots listed by bay and a size filter for finding room. Dead Shelves can now be sorted by any column, shows the date each item was last picked from its shelf (or Never), and has a Last Picked dropdown to show only never-picked items or ones idle 30–60, 60–90 or 90+ days. Empty Locations now also shows partly-full F1A and F1D shelves (they hold up to 3 items) with how many item slots are open and how much room is left, and flags shelves holding more items than allowed.
  • Ecom Backlog v1.3: The No Task tab is now called Floor Check, and its count bubble is gone so it matches the other tabs. Stuck Orders has a new Mixed Cartons section that lists any carton in Ecom reserve (R1B–R1F) holding more than one item, with its PO and ASN, so it can be split before a replen drops every item on one shelf.
  • Ecom v3.1: Turning a team member off for just one job (like Putaway) now takes their units out of that tile, the on-pace bar, the PPH and the Teams post. Before, they only greyed out in the list and their units still counted.
  • All reports: New Carrie theme — a soft baby-blue look. Pick it from the theme menu on any report or the main menu.
  • Live agents: The main 2-minute refresh (Backlog, Batches, Receiving, Totes, Tasks) now goes first when the agents are busy, so its updates come in steadier instead of sometimes running 4 to 6 minutes late.
  • All reports: Ready for the end of daylight saving on Sunday, Nov 1. Shift start times, hourly columns and "updated" times will move to standard time on their own, with nothing to change by hand. The count of waves run this shift no longer briefly includes yesterday's waves between 1:00 and 1:40 PM.
  • Shipping: Fixed an hour slip that ran all summer. The 2nd shift now counts from 2:15 PM (it was starting at 3:15 PM), and each pallet now shows in the hour it was actually built instead of the next hour. Item Prep had the same slip and is fixed too.

Thursday, Oct 1, 2026

  • Ecom Backlog v1.3: New No Task tab. Lists allocated orders that still have no pick task after an hour and says why: a short shelf, a replen that keeps cancelling, a shipped order still holding stock, or a task the system never made. Each list has a button to copy the order numbers. A Won't Fit the Shelf list shows replens that can't land because the shelf is full, with how much room is left and which item to move. Shelves with an open cycle count are flagged. Orders that have stock but no task now say why, such as waiting for the next putwall batch or arriving after the last batch of the night. Tabs can be dragged into any order, and Hospital PW is hidden for now.
  • Reserve Stock v2.2: Added the full theme menu (Solid, Pastel, Starr, Rem, Scout) so it matches the other reports.
  • Live agents: The agents now share one sign-in and take turns asking MAWM for data, so they no longer log each other out or pile up when things get busy. The EOS report was retired and its button removed from Ecom.

  • Ecom Backlog: Batches tab now shows how many order lines are in each batch.
  • All live reports: Data refresh no longer backs up, so numbers stay current during busy periods.
